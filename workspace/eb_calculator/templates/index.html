TANGEDCO Domestic Tariff Slab Info (Bi-Monthly)

Domestic Connection (Tariff 1A)

  • 0 - 100 units: Free (₹0.00 / unit) with Govt Subsidy
  • 101 - 200 units: ₹2.35 / unit
  • 201 - 400 units: ₹4.70 / unit
  • 401 - 500 units: ₹6.30 / unit
  • Above 500 units: Special slabs applicable (₹8.40, ₹9.50, ₹11.55 / unit)

Important Billing Tips

  • TANGEDCO assesses domestic power bi-monthly (every 2 months).
  • The government provides first 100 units totally free for home users.
  • Keeping bi-monthly consumption below 500 units avoids higher rate slabs.
  • Commercial & powerloom tariffs have fixed monthly charges per kW/connection.
